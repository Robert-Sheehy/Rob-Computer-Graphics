# Lamberts (Cosine) Law

This states that the intensity if the reflection of light at a surface will be proportional to the cosine of the angle of the ray of light and the normal to the surface. i.e.

<img width="867" height="428" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7d2cca8b-4834-400e-b0ef-31c7d551d581" />


$I = i_l/times k_d \times \cos(\theta) $ where $\theta$ is the angle between the ray of light and the normal of the surface
